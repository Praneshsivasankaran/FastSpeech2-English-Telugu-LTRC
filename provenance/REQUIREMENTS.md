## Evidence boundaries and release gates

- Imported data: measured source facts and file hashes are in `dataset_sources.json`; final split totals and target fingerprints are in the preparation and production audits.
- Production records distinguish actual optimizer steps from configured targets. Development smoke artifacts remain separate.
- `best.pt` retains the minimum-total-loss baseline. `selected.pt` records an explicit artifact decision; it does not necessarily minimize total validation loss or pass a quality rule. `latest.pt` retains the final saved optimizer state.
- Final English uses step 8,842 with contextual vowel targets and ordinary energy, despite the unresolved frozen duration failure. Fixed-validation recognition improves over step 7,000, but final assignment WER/CER 27.59%/11.38% does not beat the original 24.14%/8.98%. SIL zeroing improved validation but worsened assignment recognition and was rejected. Telugu retains step 2,000 after conflicting WER/CER changes. These are post-hoc decisions, not pristine blind tests or native-listener verdicts.
- Objective measurements, final ASR diagnostics and all ten objective-only observation entries are complete. The technical PDF has been generated and visually reviewed. Before publication, rerun completeness/integrity checks and verify public access to both models. No dummy audio, placeholder charts or invented listening observations satisfy a deliverable.
- Keep dataset files, gated-access credentials and the original private assignment PDF out of the public repository. Preserve dataset/code/model license notices.
- Paid compute and external publication remain subject to the user's explicit approval.

## Split review note

The allocator rejects insufficient/excessive duration, duplicate IDs and unseen validation speakers, and keeps transitive shared-text/shared-audio groups together. Whole-group allocation checks both bounds of the 10% tolerance. The seed comes from training.seed. Both measured final splits passed these gates. Public-copy sanitation is performed only in a new directory; it removes excluded locators without altering the portable metadata or production target fingerprints.
